A first estimate, not yet ground-truthed. This is one season (Jun–Nov 2024). The southern (Nellore, Prakasam) and northern (Srikakulam) grid boxes likely under-count; the dense delta belts are solid. Open-sea artefacts have been removed by excluding water bodies that run off the belt edge.

The decade trend

Ten annual estimates, 2017 to 2026. Individual years are noisy, but the trend is a clear rise of about 29% over the decade.

Read the trend line, not the individual years. The annual points swing 15 to 20% because single-season radar detection is sensitive to how many satellite passes each year had (2019 and 2022 hit the Sentinel-1B outage) and how much water sat in the ponds on the observed dates (monsoon, tides, fill cycles). Those swings are mostly observational noise; the robust signal is the upward slope. 2026 is also a partial season, June to August only. Directional evidence of real growth, not a ground-truthed annual series.

Where it stands

The heavy runs are done and running live. What is left needs data from the state side.

Decade — done

2017 to 2026 on the cluster. The trend above is a real ten-year radar series, up about 29%, not a single snapshot.

Model — done & live

Trained at scale. A U-Net on 75,000 tiles reaches F1 ~0.90 on belts it never saw, and now re-runs weekly on each new satellite pass.

Validation — next

Ground truth. Check the extent against reference imagery before it is quoted as final for enforcement.

Compliance — next

Connect the register. With the APSADA farm licences and protected-zone boundaries, the tool flags unpermitted ponds, not just detects them.
